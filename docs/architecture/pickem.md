# Pick'em

- `/pickem` (`pages/pickem/`, "Pick'em" in the nav): players predict every bracket match of a stage, the winner and the score, with one slider (`components/pickem/PickemSlider.tsx`, a tug of war whose rail takes the pointer itself, captured, since phones don't reliably send a range input's pointerup (a drag the browser takes over for scrolling puts the thumb back); a hidden range input under it serves the keyboard and screen readers: the middle is no prediction, and pulled towards a side, the further the bigger that side's win, from 5–0 at the left end through the closest results either side of the middle to 0–5 at the right; the thumb carries the score, letting go saves, letting go in the middle withdraws, and the score labels under the track and the arrow keys do the same). `pickem_predictions` holds one row per user per match with the team (not the slot, so a team a host moves out leaves the prediction `void`) and the winner's and loser's maps. Predicting needs a linked Discord account (`require_discord_verified`) and not being banned (`require_not_banned`; `can_predict` is `banned` for them, and their earlier predictions stay in the standings). The rules are in `backend/src/pickem.rs` (unit-tested) and are the server's alone; `lib/pickem.ts` only mirrors what's needed to show them.
- Windows (`pickem::windows`): only stages with bracket matches that aren't the qualifier stage have a pick'em. One opens once every match of the nearest earlier stage with matches is concluded (the qualifier lobbies for the first bracket stage) and locks as soon as any of its matches has started (its time has come, it has a room or it's concluded), `closes_at` being its first match's time. Hosts override it per stage on `/admin/stages` (`PUT /api/stages/:id/pickem`, `stages.pickem_mode`): `open` opens it now whatever the stage before it or the clock, even once its matches have begun: the clock no longer locks anything, each match locking on its own only once its room opens or it has a result (`pickem::match_locked`), so a match running late or one whose time was set in the past can still be predicted, and the stage stays open while any match is still unlocked (`closes_at` is then the next time still to come of an unlocked match, else none; `pickem::revealed` shows a locked match's split while the stage is still open, and the match page's panel calls that match locked); `closed` locks it now; `auto` follows the rule. A match can be predicted while its stage is open, both teams are known, the stage has a best of (the winner's maps are its `first_to`) and it isn't locked (by the rule, once it has started: its time has come, it has a room or it's concluded); one that gets its teams after the lock is never predicted.
- Scoring (`pickem::points`): 3 for the winner and the score, 2 for the winner only, 1 for the score only (the figures, whoever won: 5-3 is right for a 3-5 too), else 0; a forfeit has no score, so only its winner scores. Standings rank by points, then exact predictions, then winners; level on points and exact share a rank.
- Privacy: nobody sees how a match was predicted until its stage locks (or, in a stage a host opened, until its room opens or it has a result). `GET /api/public/pickem` sends every stage's window, the viewer's own predictions with their points, the split (`team1`/`team2` counts and every score's count) of each match of a locked stage, and the standings (overall and per stage). `GET /api/public/pickem/matches/:id` feeds the public match page's panel (`pages/match/MatchPickem.tsx`, under the score sheet): the ballot while it's open (with how many predicted so far), then only the winner split, one bar in the sides' colours with each side's percentage, and the viewer's pick and points. The score histogram (`PickemScoreChart` in `components/pickem/PickemSplit.tsx`, on the slider's axis, the result and the viewer's pick marked) is on the pick'em page, under each locked match's split behind "Skor dağılımı"; the match page's panel links there with `/pickem?match=<id>` (`pickemMatchPath`), which shows the match's stage, opens its distribution and scrolls to it. An open stage's match is a ballot, like the match page's: no teams line or score box, only the slider with the teams named at its ends (`is-ballot`; `renderName` gives each its banner on its inner side as in the bracket list, dropped in a narrow list, and makes it a `TeamPopover`, as every team name on the page is), whose thumb is the one place the pick shows. A locked stage's match has the teams with the match's own score between them (its live or final score, else "vs") and says the pick in words at the row's end ("Senin tahminin 5–3", under its points once it has a result; on a phone on a line of its own under them), as the match page does. A match still waiting on its pairings (`isUndecided`) is predicted as each of its potential matches, grouped under it as the bracket list hangs them (`components/pickem/PickemPotentials.tsx`): a head line with its code, an "N olası eşleşme" chip and "Daha kesinleşmedi", a note that only the pairing actually played counts, and the pairings branching off a rail, each led by an "Olası" chip and its own time in place of the code; the group is placed by its earliest pairing and carries the bracket match's `pickem-<id>`, so `/pickem?match=` finds it. The page shows the ballot beside the standings rail; under 960px they're two tabs ("Tahminler", "Puan durumu"). The stage track is the site's `StageTabs` (its `mark`: open lit, locked ruled in ink, finished in muted ink, the viewer's points under a locked stage), so on a phone it scrolls sideways with the shown stage in the middle. It follows the bracket live like `/bracket`; predictions push nothing.

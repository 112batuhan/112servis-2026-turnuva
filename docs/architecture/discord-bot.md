# Discord bot (`backend/src/discord`)

- Runs only when `DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN` and `DISCORD_GUILD_ID` are set (`DiscordBotConfig`, flattened into `Config`); `AppState.discord` is then `Some`. Everything it points at in the server (the Verified and Player roles, the team/match announcement, results, host, referee and streamer channels, and the open and archive categories for qualifier and bracket match channels) is picked by hosts on `/admin/discord` and stored in the single-row `discord_settings` (`db/discord.rs`); unset means the bot skips whatever needs it. Hosts can also switch it off and on there (`PUT /api/discord/active`, `discord_settings.bot_active`, mirrored in memory on `Bot`): while off the gateway is closed, the worker and the scheduler wait, `Discord::bot()`/`enabled()` act as if it weren't set up (so nothing is queued; `configured()` still reads the server for the settings page), and switching off drops the jobs still queued; switching on reconnects, and the Ready's `SyncAll` catches up. The same page can pause match channels (`PUT /api/discord/channels/paused`, `discord_settings.match_channels_paused`: no new channel opens, existing ones still follow their match; unpausing queues a `SyncMatch` for every match that needs one) and delete every match channel (`POST /api/discord/channels/delete`, a `DeleteMatchChannels` job, exempt from `JOB_TIMEOUT`: the ones on record, then any other text channel in the match categories carrying the bot's own member overwrite, such as a deleted match's archived one, then the overflow categories left empty; unless paused, the matches that need one get new ones). It needs the privileged Server Members intent. Uses serenity (gateway + HTTP).
- Handlers never call Discord. They queue `discord::jobs::Job`s with `state.discord.enqueue(conn, &[...])` in the same transaction as the change (`discord_outbox`, woken by `pg_notify` on commit; nothing is queued while the bot is off), and one worker (`jobs.rs`) runs them in id order, retrying failures with backoff and dropping ones Discord refuses (4xx). The bot's HTTP client (`http_client` in `discord/mod.rs`) has connect/request timeouts, which serenity's lacks, and logs rate-limit waits of 2 s or more ("discord rate limit, waiting"). Serenity waits out rate limits by sleeping (a channel can be renamed twice per 10 minutes), so a job that runs over `JOB_TIMEOUT` (60 s; not `SyncAll`) is retried later instead of holding up the rest, and a channel rename is its own call waited on for at most `RENAME_WAIT` (5 s), recorded as not done when it times out so the next sync retries it. A message Discord refuses to delete is given up on with a warning, and a results post still on record is replaced, so a reopened match always gets its new post. Sync jobs reconcile Discord with the database: `SyncUser`/`SyncMember` (roles: Verified for a linked account, Player for a team member), `SyncMatch` (the match's channel), `SyncAll` (who's in the server, every member's roles, every match channel; queued on every gateway Ready, on saving settings and from the admin page; a member whose roles Discord refuses to change is logged and skipped, so one doesn't stop the rest). Event jobs (`TeamFormed`, `Rescheduled`, `Reminder`, …) are rendered by `messages.rs` (Turkish, like the site) into one `Post` job per destination, so a failed send retries alone. When adding a site action that Discord should know about, queue the job next to the website notification.
- Linking Discord (`handlers/auth.rs`) asks for `guilds.join` while the bot runs and adds the account to the server. `discord_accounts.in_guild` tracks membership (null until checked); the gateway's member add/remove events and `SyncAll` keep it current. A team member going from in to out gets a `discord_left` notification to their teammate and the hosts plus Discord messages (likewise a new ban queues `UserBanned`, read when it runs and skipped if the ban was lifted by then: an embed in the host channel with the player, their team, who banned them and why, a DM to the player with the reason and what it stops, and a DM to their teammate without the reason); while out, they can't invite or accept (`require_in_server` in `handlers/team.rs`), and `/team` and `/admin/teams` show it.
- Match channels (`channels.rs`, `match_discord`): a bracket match gets one once it has both teams and a time, a qualifier lobby once a team signs up; an unconcluded match that loses a team again (reopening the match it came from takes it back out) has its channel deleted (`should_close`), and gets a new one once it has both again; visible to its players, referee and streamer (only accounts known to be in the server), hidden from everyone else (hosts are admins). An hour after a match is concluded (`ARCHIVE_AFTER_SECS` in `db/discord.rs`, so the players can still talk) it turns read-only and moves to the archive category: `MatchInfo.archived` is computed with the database's clock, and the scheduler queues the `SyncMatch` (`take_due_archives`, `match_discord.archived_for`); reopening moves it back; deleting the match archives it at once. The "concluded" and "reopened" posts in the channel replace each other (`Job::Post.status_of`, `match_discord.status_message_id`), so a reopened match doesn't keep its old result there. `match_discord.synced` records what was last applied, so an unchanged sync makes no API calls; syncs replace the channel's permission overwrites. The same sync keeps the channel's pinned card (`messages::match_card`: time, stage, teams, staff, rooms, result, and for a bracket match both teams' banners side by side; Discord shows the images of embeds sharing the main embed's url as one gallery, `OutMessage.gallery`, and banners are only linked when `FRONTEND_URL` is https, since Discord fetches them; the 15-minute announcement shows them too, and a banner change queues a `SyncMatch` for the team's open matches) in step: posted with the channel, pinging everyone in the match, then edited whenever it renders differently (`match_discord.card_message_id`/`card_synced`), so anything that changes what it shows has to queue a `SyncMatch`. Every change is also posted in the channel as plain text that pings the match's players, referee and streamer (`Pings::everyone`); only the card, the posts in the staff and host channels and the DMs are embeds, with anyone an embed should ping mentioned in its content (`OutMessage::embed`), since mentions inside an embed never ping. A full category (50) gets a sibling category created next to it (`discord_overflow_categories`).
- `scheduler.rs` (every 30 s): a reminder in the match channel an hour before a match, and the public announcement 15 minutes before a bracket match, pinging its `match_subscriptions` (spectators toggle them on `/bracket`). `match_discord.reminded_for`/`announced_for` hold the start time each was sent for, so a moved match gets them again. The public announcement is laid out like the match card (`messages::match_fields`, shared by both, naming people without pinging them) and kept up to date after it goes out (`discord/announcement.rs`, run by every `SyncMatch`; `match_discord.announce_message_id`/`announce_synced`, compared like the card): the live score while the match has a room, "ertelendi" with the new time once it's moved (the next announcement for the new time deletes the old message), and "iptal edildi" once it loses a team or is deleted. A score saved for a match that was never announced for its current time (it was already past when the scheduler could have) posts the announcement then (`claim_late_announcement`, claimed like the scheduler's, so the two can't both post it) (`Job::AnnouncementCancelled` carries what the message needs, since the match is gone). A concluded match, forfeits included, has its announcement deleted, since its result goes to the results channel. Concluding a bracket match posts its results in the results channel from the site's data only (the map results the referee page saved, with each player's mods on FM/TB maps, the hand-awarded ones, bans, picks, with the rooms linked but never read from osu!); reopening deletes the message.
- Referees and streamers get confirm-time requests as DMs with buttons that call `services::notifications::answer_as`; a declined slot is offered in the referee or streamer channel, pinging everyone its `staff_declined` notifications went to (`declined_recipients`). The referee page pings a player in the match channel when their Discord name is clicked (`POST /api/matches/:id/ping`, 30 s cooldown per player). Team announcements skip a team when both its players were announced in the last 7 days (`discord_announced_players`).
- Slash commands (`commands.rs`: `/rank`, `/maclar`, `/takim`, `/gecmis`, `/mac`) are registered to the guild on every Ready.
- The auto referee (bancho-bots.md): while the hosts have rooms opening by themselves (`app_settings.auto_rooms`), 20 minutes before a match in auto mode that has no room yet, its channel pings the players that the bot invites them in 10 minutes and that `-davet` resends it (`Job::AutorefReminder`, from the scheduler, `match_discord.autoref_reminded_for`); a room it opens is announced naming its bot (`RoomAdded { bot }`); a player's `-rapor` or `-durdur` and a handover post an embed in the referee channel pinging the hosts and the match's referee, or every referee while it has none (`Job::AutorefAlert`, `db::discord::alert_recipients`); a no-show DMs the absent team's players (`Job::NoShow`), and a lobby's channel is told which team was taken out.

# Public match page

- `/match/:matchId` (`pages/public/match/`) is the referee page's cockpit for everyone, live: the pool with the draft (`PoolView`), the room bar, the map clock (`MapClock`), the players in their slots with their mods and scores (`PlayersView`), the score sheet (`ScoreSheet`) and the room's chat (`MatchChat`), with the same phone tabs (`useCockpit`, `CockpitTabs`). Those pieces live in `components/match/` (`Cockpit`, `PoolView`, `PlayersView`, `ScoreSheet`, `MapClock`, `ChatRow`, each with the cockpit stylesheet of its panel) and are shared with the referee page, whose wrappers in `pages/admin/referee/` fill in its controls (Discord pings, invite/move, ban/pick buttons, "Sayma", hand-awarded results, forfeits); both pages follow their match with `lib/useMatchFeed.ts` (load, the live socket, the polling fallback, and answers that arrive out of order). It reads `GET /api/public/matches/:id` (`PublicMatch` in `handlers/referee.rs`: no referee notes, players without Discord, the pool only once its stage is published) and `GET /api/public/matches/:id/chat`, then follows the live socket (`lib/useMatchPage.ts`, which the overlay reads its match with too): only while the match is live (a room, not concluded) does it `watch` the match and get `chat` lines as they come (and poll while the socket is down); a concluded match shows its saved chat. `score`, `match` and `bracket` reload the match either way, so a reopened match goes live again and an upcoming one starts streaming once its room is added. The room's state is the bots' reading of it (`live` in the response, then each `room` event; `lib/liveRoom.ts` shapes it for both pages: `lobbyOf`, `finishedOf`, `currentEntryOf`, `rosterOf`); a bracket match's sheet and score are what the site saved (`savedTally`, which the room's bot keeps up to date), a lobby's maps are the bots' reading of the chat. A match the bots don't run (concluded, or the manager is restarting) gets the same view read from its saved chat (`bancho::static_view`). Until it's concluded the public page heads each team in `PlayersView` with its banner (`banners`). Once a match is concluded the room's state is left out: the public page shows the teams as `TeamCard`s (the teams page's ruled cards, each ruled in its side's colour) and drops the map clock and the pool's "Odada" mark; the referee page's `PlayersView` (`finished`) lists them as a roster without slots, ready states, mods or who's in the room. "Sadeleştir" in the chat (on by default, in localStorage) hides `isRoomNoise` lines (`lib/bancho.ts`: BanchoBot's housekeeping, each player's "finished playing" and `!mp` commands; maps set, started, finished and rolls stay).
- Links to it (`components/MatchPageLink.tsx`, `matchPagePath`/`hasMatchPage`/`fixturePath` in `lib/bracket.ts`): every bracket match with both teams and every lobby. Fixture rows lead to it (a live one says "Canlı takip et"), and grid cards, calendar chips, list rows, the public bracket's selection bar, lobby cards, the team page's lobby and the admin match panel carry the link, frost while the match is live ("Canlı takip et"). On `/admin/*` the link renders nothing for those allowed Referee (referees and hosts), who open the referee page instead. Discord's match card and announcement have a "Maç sayfası" field, every match embed's title links to it (`messages::match_page_url`), and so do the "maç bitti" post and the results post.

# Teams (2v2)

- `teams` / `team_members` (a player's `user_id` is the primary key, so one team per player) / `team_invites`. A team is created with both players when an invite is accepted, and doing so drops every other invite involving either of them.
- Eligibility is `team_rank.rs`: each rank maps to a value and the pair's sum must stay at or below `LIMIT`. It's checked when inviting and again when accepting (`handlers/team.rs`).
- The inviter names the team on the invite; the name moves to `teams.name` on accept and is unique case-insensitively (`teams_name_key`). Either player can rename or disband their team, and hosts can rename or disband any team.
- Banners live in `team_banners` as bytes (small app, no object storage). An upload is never stored as sent (`backend/src/banner.rs`, unit-tested): it's capped at 2 MB, has to start with a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP's magic bytes and be at most 4096 pixels a side (4096×2048 in all), and is then decoded and encoded again (a JPEG or opaque WebP as JPEG, the rest as PNG), so the stored copy carries no EXIF and nothing behind the image. An animated GIF stays animated: its frames are written again as they are (palettes, pixels, timing) into a new GIF, at most 300 frames and 64M pixels of canvas × frames; an animated WebP keeps its first frame. A team can upload once per 15 s (`banner::Cooldowns`, in memory). The banner is served with `nosniff` and a sandboxing CSP, and `Team.banner_url` carries the upload time as a version so the image can be cached forever.
- Ranks come from osu! through `rank_refresh.rs`, using the bulk `GET /users` endpoint (50 ids per call, osu!standard stats, no country rank). The player search, the team page, inviting and accepting refresh the users involved when their rank is over 10 minutes old (`osu_users.rank_updated_at`); failures fall back to the stored ranks. Every team member is refreshed daily (a background task checks hourly against `app_settings.ranks_updated_at`, so restarts don't skip or repeat it) and hosts can trigger it from the admin panel. Formed teams that no longer fit get a `rank_warning`; nothing is changed automatically.
- `app_settings.teams_frozen` (toggled by hosts) blocks every player-side team change: inviting, accepting, renaming, banners and disbanding (`require_unfrozen` in `handlers/team.rs`). It also freezes ranks: no daily, manual or on-demand refresh (`rank_refresh.rs`), and logging in keeps the stored rank. Cancelling or declining invites and all host actions still work.
- Only users with no role or just Streamer play (`Roles::can_play`); other staff are filtered out of the search and can't invite or accept. Banned users (`auth-and-roles.md`) are filtered out too, and can't invite, accept or change their team. Inviting and accepting also need a linked Discord account.
- The team lists (`/api/public/teams`, `/api/teams`) carry each team's place in the qualifier (`qualifier: { rank, pending, eliminated }`, `eliminated` once a bracket exists without the team, shown as a red rank, `services::stats::qualifier_places`); the public list only once the qualifier's stage is published, like `/stats`. The cards show it under the team value ("Eleme sıralaması #7", with any team warning under it), the rows as an "Eleme" column once any team has a place. The team popovers (`TeamPopover`, opened from a team's name on the bracket, calendar, pick'em, lobbies and statistics) show it the same way, read from `GET /api/public/qualifier-places` (kept in the stats cache under its own key; `lib/useQualifierPlaces.ts` reads it once for every popover, at most once a minute, starting when a name is pointed at or focused, and the card waits for it before it's placed so it doesn't grow afterwards).

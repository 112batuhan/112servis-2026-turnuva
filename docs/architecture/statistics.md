# Statistics

- `/stats` (`pages/stats/`, "İstatistik" in the nav) shows every published stage's statistics, counted from the scores the site saved: the stage track (`GET /api/public/stats`, each stage's matches with a saved map and those live; without `?stage=` it opens the one being played, else the latest with scores), then five tabs in the URL (`?tab=`): Mappool (`PoolStats.tsx`: picks, bans and how often the picker won in a bracket stage, how many teams played it in the qualifier, the field's mean and σ, the best player's and team's score), each map's player and team leaderboards (`MapBoard.tsx`, `?map=`), and the player and team standings (`Standings.tsx`: sortable and searchable, or a map matrix with `?view=matrix`, whose cells show Skor by default, then Accuracy, Z or Sıra). In a bracket stage the matrix's "Pick ve banlar" toggle (off by default, `?drafts=1` when on) has each cell show what was drafted on that map in the row's team's matches, from `drafts` on each standing (`stats::DraftTally`, a player's are their team's): a played map's figure takes its picker's colour, `--color-draft-team` (mavi) for the team's own pick and `--color-draft-opponent` (kırmızı) for its opponent's (the tiebreaker, picked by nobody, stays in ink), and a banned map's empty cell a small dot in its banner's colour, with a legend over the matrix while it's on. The tooltip names every pick and ban, a ban of a map played in another match included. What a stage's statistics are counted from, and the count, are `services/stats.rs` (`stage_pool`, `count`), which the seeding page and the team lists use for the qualifier too. `GET /api/public/stats/:id` (`handlers/stats.rs`) only serves published stages (checked on every request). What it counts is kept as JSON in `StatsCache` (`stats_cache.rs`, `AppState.stats_cache`, per stage, the stage activity under the nil id) until the live hub hears of any change (`spawn_invalidation` on `Live::changes`) or 60 s pass (pool edits and team renames push nothing); a stage is counted by one request at a time and the rest wait for its result, so a saved map costs one count however many pages are open. The page reloads after `score`, `bracket` and `match` events and every minute while visible (`useStageStats.ts`); switching stages keeps the last one on screen until the next is in (`lib/useStageCache.ts`, see `frontend.md`).
- The numbers are `backend/src/stats.rs` (pure, unit-tested): every play is measured against the field that played the same map, its Z = (score − mean) / sample σ (0 when the field can't say), teams by their players' summed score, players by their own. A bracket stage counts every play (a map played in two matches counts twice) and ranks by the average Z. The qualifier decides the seeding: a team's latest attempt at a map counts, teams are ranked by the sum of their Z over the pool, and once its lobby is concluded a team (or player) without a score on a map gets the Z a score of 0 would have, without counting in that map's mean and σ (so a no-show doesn't move anyone else's Z); a team whose lobby isn't over is `pending` and its missing maps don't count yet.
- Every match's saved maps are in `match_map_results` / `match_map_scores` (keyed to `matches`): a bracket match's with each team's total, a qualifier lobby's without (`team1_score`/`team2_score` null; every counted attempt in order, "Sayma" respected), all written through `db::bracket::replace_saved_maps`. A lobby's are written by the room's bot whenever they differ from the site's (`count_lobby` in `bancho/manager.rs`, `tally::lobby_rows`, `db::stats::replace_qualifier_results`; the first reading included, nobody enters them by hand). A concluded lobby without saved maps is read from its saved chat by `bancho::backfill_lobbies` at startup and after a dev reseed. The bracket readers (referee page, Discord results, the bots' context) only ask for bracket matches.
- The chat has no accuracy, so `room_scores.rs` reads osu!'s record of a match's rooms (`osu_api::matches`, `accuracy`) 8 s after each save, and sweeps every minute for rooms whose match has a saved score still without it (two minutes after a read, then twice as long after each read that changed nothing, `match_rooms.scores_read_at` / `scores_misses`, rooms of the last two weeks). `db::stats::apply_room_accuracy` writes it onto `match_map_scores.accuracy`: the record's score by the same player on the same beatmap with the same score, or, when the player has only the one on that beatmap on both sides, that one. It pushes `match` when it changed any. The bots rewrite a match's rows on every save, so `replace_saved_maps` keeps the accuracy of every score that stays the same (player, map and score).
- `StatsCache` also keeps `GET /api/public/bracket` (`AppState.bracket_cache`, 10 s at most, since team renames and banners push nothing), invalidated the same way: one read per change however many tabs reload. Only `{matches, teams}` is cached; the response adds the viewer's `my_team_id` (`stats_cache::with_field`).

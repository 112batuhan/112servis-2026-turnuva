# Dev login and the seed

For testing, set `DEV_LOGIN=true` in `.env`: the backend then mounts `/api/dev/*` (`handlers/dev.rs`) and the frontend bundles a floating account switcher (`components/DevSwitcher.tsx`, behind the build-time `__DEV_LOGIN__` constant from `vite.config.ts`) with buttons to reseed or clear dummy data. Dummy accounts have negative `osu_id`s (stages can't be marked that way, so the seed reuses stages by name and `clear.sql` only deletes seeded ones that have no categories); the seed lives in `backend/seed/`; every made-up score comes from `model.sql`'s temporary functions, which run before `seed.sql` (and again before `qualifiers.sql`), and `clear.sql` also removes every bracket match fed through `bracket_links` by a removed one, so a bracket made from the dummies goes whole (its timeline has one stage being played at a time, each stage a week in which no team plays more than twice: every qualifier lobby is played the week before the bracket, the Round of 32 and Round of 16 are over and the Quarterfinals (winners round 3, losers rounds 2 and 3) are the one stage with live matches, everything after upcoming (Semifinals, Finals, then the Grand Final with its bracket reset W33); L20 waits on L15 (live) and L16 (upcoming), so the seed makes its four potential matches itself, with an agreed time, staff changes, a pending proposal and predictions; it also gives the first qualifier lobby seven teams and a chat that plays the whole qualifier pool (the first map twice), W1 two rooms with saved chat, the first live winners match a chat stopped mid-way through its freemod map, the first live losers match a chat at 4-4 after eight picks with the tiebreaker due (bans, picks and saved results too), every concluded match saved map results with player scores and mods (a 5-4 one ends on TB) and its own picks and bans (W1 the ones its chat plays, every other match a shuffle of its stage's pool, picked in turn with two bans, each team winning its own picks up to the score), every seeded score from one model (`pg_temp.seed_score`, made at the top of `seed.sql` and dropped at its end: each map its own centre and spread, each player a skill that holds across maps, each play its own luck and now and then a choke, bent softly under 1M; in a bracket match the losing side's total scaled to finish short of the winner's, so chat, saved results and statistics agree), every qualifier lobby saved results (the first one's matching its chat, the other three without chat, Rank Uçtu missing the pool's last two maps so the statistics show the zero rule), every seeded saved score an accuracy, the first two upcoming matches some proposed times with the players' notices, Rank Uçtu a rank-limit notice, Cezerye (of Yol Hediyesi) a ban by DevHost with its two notifications (Cezerye's and teammate Pestil's), every seeded bracket stage a best of if it has none (Bo9 up to the quarterfinals, Bo11 from the semifinals, Bo13 in the grand final; the first four stages' pools published) and a pick and ban order if it has none (`p1-p2-b2-b1-p1-*` up to the quarterfinals, `p1-p2-b2-b1-b2-b1-p1-*` from the semifinals; the seeded bans and picks follow them), every seeded stage the pool categories it's missing (by name), filled with real ranked maps added by DevPooler, and the pick'em: the Semifinals' opened by hand (`pickem_mode = 'open'`, which `clear.sql` hands back to `auto`) and 45 dummies' predictions for every seeded match of the first four bracket stages with both teams, each with a skill so the standings spread; `clear.sql` deletes the chat of the matches it removes and the categories holding only dummy-added maps) (also runnable with `psql "$DATABASE_URL" -f backend/seed/clear.sql -f backend/seed/model.sql -f backend/seed/seed.sql`, minus the team banners in `seed/banners/`, which `db/dev.rs` adds) and must be kept in step with schema changes. "Reseed + live match" (`POST /api/dev/seed/live`, shown to a real account) reseeds and then runs `seed/live.sql`, which teams the signed-in account with a dummy against a dummy team in a bracket match of the Quarterfinals starting now with no room, refereed by DevReferee, so a real osu! room can be run with the real player in it (with an active bot on `/admin/bancho`). "Reseed: qualifiers done" (`POST /api/dev/seed/qualifiers`) reseeds and then runs `seed/qualifiers.sql` (after `model.sql` again): the tournament just after its qualifier, for `/admin/seeding`. seed.sql's bracket goes, eight more teams join (40), and 39 of them have played one of six concluded lobbies (A-F; lobby A keeps its chat, the rest get results from the model, every pool map once): Rank Uçtu misses the last two maps, Gece Kuşları didn't show up (every map a 0), Kayıp Rank never signed up (no place) and Yol Hediyesi's banned Cezerye puts that team last, so a Round of 32 leaves 8 teams out and a Round of 16 24. "Reseed: teams only" (`POST /api/dev/seed/teams`) runs `clear.sql` and `seed/teams.sql` instead of the seed: the dev staff (DevHost to DevYayinci), seven teams (five with banners, Rank Uçtu over the limit, Kayıp Rank with an unranked player) and an open invite between two free players, with no lobby, score, stage or bracket, so the team lists show no qualifier places. It defaults to off. It works over https too, so testers can use it on the live server while the site isn't public, but anyone who can reach the site can then sign in as any account (hosts included), so it must be turned off before launch.

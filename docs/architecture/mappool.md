# Map pool

The schema has changed a lot across migrations; the current model is:

- `stages` (ordered by `position`, with a `published` flag) → `stage_categories` (ordered, with a `color` `#rrggbb`, a short name and a kind) → `category_slots` (ordered, with `editor_notes`).
- A category's `abbreviation` (1-6 capital letters, `^[A-Z]{1,6}$`; unique within the stage, checked by the handler: `PoolError::BadCategoryAbbreviation`, `CategoryAbbreviationTaken`) labels its slots: "NM1", "NM2", or just "TB" for a category of one slot (`pool_entries` in `bancho/tally/pool.rs`, `slotLabel` in `lib/category.ts`, the Discord results' `db::discord::stage_maps`). Those labels are what players type in the room to ban and pick, and what every page, the overlay and Discord call the maps; the category's `name` is only its heading. Its `kind` (`category_kind`: `nomod`, `modded`, `freemod`, `tiebreaker`; `CategoryKind` in `backend/src/category.rs`, mirrored in `lib/category.ts`) carries the rules, never the name: a team may pick from a NoMod category twice in a row, Freemod and Tiebreaker maps are played with Freemod (an FM map needs one of HD, HR, EZ, FL per team, bancho-bots.md), and the tiebreaker is neither banned nor picked. Both are set when a category is created on the editor's stage settings (`POST /api/mappool/stages/:id/categories` with `name`, `abbreviation`, `kind`; typing NM, FM or TB picks the kind they mean until the kind is chosen by hand) and changed in its row (`PATCH /api/mappool/categories/:id`), which pushes `bracket` so the bots read the new labels and rules. Migration 0070 set them for existing categories from their names (a spelled-out mod became its acronym, any other name its letters).
- Routes: everything about a stage's pool is under `/api/mappool` (`handlers/mappool.rs`): `GET /api/mappool/stages/:id` (the editor's view), `PUT …/stages/:id/published`, `…/stages/:id/pack`, `POST …/stages/:id/categories`, `/api/mappool/categories/…` (reorder, rename/recolour, delete, add a slot), `/api/mappool/slots/:id`, `POST /api/mappool/maps` (add to the generic pool) and `/api/mappool/maps/:id[/notes|/stats]`; the public pool is `GET /api/public/mappool/stages/:id`. The stage as a round stays under `/api/stages` (`handlers/stages.rs`): listing (`GET /api/stages`, behind the pool's `view_guard`; `GET /api/public/stages`, every stage published or not, as `stage_track::PublicStage`: no draft order or pack link, with its `played` and `live` match counts and its `state` on the tournament's track), and the host-only create, rename (`PATCH /api/stages/:id`, name only), reorder, delete, qualifier, abbreviation, best of and draft order, plus the pick'em mode (`handlers/pickem.rs`).
- At most one stage has `is_qualifier` (partial unique index `stages_qualifier_key`): the map pool the qualifier lobbies play. Hosts pick it on `/admin/stages` (`PUT /api/stages/qualifier`); bracket matches point at their stage with `bracket_matches.stage_id` instead.
- `stages.best_of` (odd, 1-25, null until set) is how many maps the stage's bracket matches are played to; hosts set it per stage on `/admin/stages` (`PUT /api/stages/:id/best-of`, which queues a `SyncMatch` for the stage's matches and pushes `bracket`). It rides along on `RoomStage`/`MatchStage` and Discord's `MatchInfo`, and every view that names a match's stage says it (`stageLabel` in `lib/bestOf.ts`, `messages::stage_label`: "Quarterfinals · Bo9"). `standingOf` (`lib/bestOf.ts`, mirroring `standing_of` in `bancho/tally/standings.rs`) says where a score stands against it (open, match point, tiebreaker, won, over); the score sheet shows it (below). It never concludes a match or blocks concluding: that's the referee's call.
- `stages.draft_order` (null until set) is the order its bracket matches ban and pick in, written by hosts on `/admin/stages` (`PUT /api/stages/:id/draft-order`, which pushes `bracket`) as steps joined by dashes: `p1-p2-b2-b1-p1-*` (`p` a map picked and played, `b` a ban; 1 is the team whose ban or pick the referee marks first, which the roll decides, 2 the other; a closing `*` has picks take turns from there, each to the team that didn't make the mark before). `backend/src/draft.rs` (unit-tested) parses it, stores it normalized and says whose turn it is (`next_turn`, against the bans and picks in the order they were marked, by `created_at`, and the turns that ran out on the auto referee's clock, `match_missed_turns`: a missed ban takes its step with no map, a missed pick takes none and passes that step to the other team, and the first turn, made or missed, says who 1 is); `lib/draftOrder.ts` mirrors it. A stage without one plays `DEFAULT_ORDER` (`b1-b2-p2-*`: two bans, then picks in turn from the team that didn't ban first). It rides on `RoomStage` (always filled in, the default when unset).
- `stages.abbreviation` (1-6 characters, null until set; `PoolError::AbbreviationTooLong` past that) is the stage's short name ("QF", "RO32"). Hosts type it in the small field after the name on `/admin/stages` (`PUT /api/stages/:id/abbreviation`, empty takes it off; pushes `bracket`, no Discord job since no card shows it). It rides along on `MatchStage`, and the bracket board writes it on each match card's head after the code (`BracketMatchCard`, an `<abbr>` whose title is `stageLabel`: "Quarterfinals · Bo9"), so a card says its stage wherever the board is scrolled; the stage labels over each column stay.
- `stages.pack_url` (null until set) links the stage's beatmap pack, which is hosted elsewhere (the site stores no files). Map poolers set it in the map pool editor's stage settings (`PUT /api/mappool/stages/:id/pack`, http(s) only), and the public `/mappool` page shows "Toplu indir" in the stage bar for the stage being viewed. The stage bar is `components/StageTabs.tsx`, the site's stage track (also on `/stats`, `/pickem`, the bracket list and the editor; a page can pass its own `mark` for each stage's rule and note): a grid that wraps where it's wide enough, and on a phone (≤720px) one line that scrolls sideways like the landing's, keeping the shown stage in the middle (`lib/useCentredItem.ts`). On `/mappool` it shows the published stages of `/api/public/stages` (the page reads nothing else for them), lights the one whose `state` is `current` and says how many matches each has being played (`live`). The track's rule is only in `backend/src/stage_track.rs` (pure, unit-tested; its rows from `db::stages::list_public_stages` and `tournament_progress`): the qualifier while lobbies are open and no bracket match is done, else the stage of the earliest unfinished bracket match; stages before it are done, as is any finished one. The landing page's track is the same list in `StageTabs`' display mode (no `onSelect`: no buttons, the current stage centred), unpublished stages included (their names and best of were public through the bracket's matches anyway); `/stats` and `/mappool` drop the unpublished ones. `played`/`live` are counted as `db::stats::stage_activity` counts them. The pack link comes with the pool (`/api/public/mappool/stages/:id`), not the stage list, so "Toplu indir" is the pack of the pool on screen. Without `?stage=` the page opens on that current stage, else one being played, else the latest with scores, else the first; it's chosen once, and the lit stage and live counts reload on the live socket's bracket changes.
- `pool_maps` holds each map **self-contained**: the beatmap metadata plus the mod-adjusted stats are copied in when the map is added, and there is no beatmap cache table. `slot_id IS NULL` means the map is in the global **generic pool** shared by all stages. Otherwise it fills exactly one slot (`UNIQUE(slot_id)`). Deleting a slot sends its map back to the generic pool. The same beatmap under different mods is a separate row.
- Mods are a concatenated 2-letter acronym string (`"HDDT"`) stored on the map, chosen from the editor's list when it's added (`AddMapForm`; the handler refuses anything else, `PoolError::BadMods`). The list also has **FM** and **TB**: a map added for a freemod or tiebreaker slot. Such a map is the beatmap without mods everywhere its stats and its `!mp mods` are concerned (`category::playable_mods`), is played with Freemod (and a TB map is the tiebreaker) wherever it sits (`PoolEntry.freemod`/`tiebreaker`, `playsFreemod`/`isTiebreaker` in `lib/category.ts`), and shows "FM"/"TB" as its mods on the card. Migration 0070 marked the mod-less maps of existing FM and TB categories so. Stats are fixed when the map is added (`resolve_stats` in `handlers/mappool.rs`): only SR comes from the osu! difficulty-attributes API (skipped for nomod; the API no longer returns AR/OD), and CS/AR/OD/BPM/length are computed in `mods.rs` (unit-tested; AR and OD go through their timing windows, so DT AR 10 is 11). A map without mods (NoMod, FM or TB) also stores its stats under HR (`hr_star_rating`, `hr_cs`, `hr_ar`, `hr_od`; null for maps added with mods), which `MapCard` shows as a second "HR" line under the nomod one for an FM map, or a map without mods in a Freemod category (`showsHrStats` in `lib/category.ts`; not TB), on the editor (the shared pool too, where an FM map goes by its own mark) and the public page, with BPM and length centred between the two lines and the FM mark in front of the NM / HR heads, between the lines too. The card and its stat line are size containers: the stats close up (and step down a size under 310px) so the two lines fit on phones, and a card under 520px stacks title and meta beside the cover with the stats full-width beneath. They're never recomputed on their own: the editor's refresh button on a map card (`POST /api/mappool/maps/:id/stats`) resolves them again, and an FM map without HR stats says so there with a "Yeniden hesapla" link. HP is not stored.
- Public vs editor views: `editor_notes` (on slots and maps) must never reach the `/api/public/*` routes. The public pool route uses separate `Public*` row structs and only return published stages. Keep that split when adding fields.
- osu! API calls (`osu_api/`) use an app token from the client-credentials flow, cached in memory in `AppState.osu_app_token`.

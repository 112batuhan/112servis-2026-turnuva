# Bancho bots (`backend/src/bancho`)

- Layout: `mod.rs` is the `Bancho` handle the rest of the site uses (`RoomCommand`, `BotStatus`, `spawn`). `names.rs` compares osu! usernames the way Bancho writes them. `connection/` speaks to Bancho (`irc.rs`: lines, channels, the login; `bot.rs`: one account's connection and its pacing). Pool category rules (freemod, tiebreaker, NoMod picks twice in a row) come from each category's kind, the crate's `category.rs` (mappool.md). `room/` is one multiplayer room: its state read from BanchoBot's lines (`mod.rs`, with `parse.rs` and `mods.rs`) and the `!mp` commands sent to it (`commands.rs`). `context.rs` is what the bots know of a match, `tally/` counts it (`pool.rs`: which pool map a beatmap was; `results.rs`: the finished maps, from the chat and osu!; `count.rs`: the score and the saved rows; `standings.rs`: who's ahead and whose turn it is) and `view.rs` is the `LiveRoom` every page reads. `manager/` is the one task that runs everything: `mod.rs` holds its state and loop and each other file is one part of `impl Manager` (`bots`, `runs`, `events` (what the bots hear), `making`, `automation`, `auto_referee`, `timers`, `requests` (what the pages ask)), with `saving.rs` (the ordered writer tasks) and `offline.rs` (reading a match without the manager). `autoref/` is the auto referee: `mod.rs` holds its types and `Snapshot`, and each other file is one part of `impl AutoRef` (`clock`, `warmups`, `draft`, `map`, `banchobot`, `players`, `resume`, `view`), its tests in `tests.rs`.

- Everything that speaks to Bancho is here: the site's bots. Nobody on the site connects to Bancho with their own account; referees and hosts run the rooms and talk in them through the bots (referee-page.md). The site's own osu! accounts make, run and log every multiplayer room. Hosts add them on `/admin/bancho` (`pages/admin/BanchoAdminPage.tsx`; `handlers/bancho.rs`: `GET`/`POST /api/bancho/bots`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/bancho/bots/:id`) with the account's IRC password (`bancho_bots`; write-only, never sent back) and switch each on or off; every active one stays connected (`connection/bot.rs`: logs in, answers PINGs, reconnects with backoff, and waits for a new password after Bancho refuses one). The page shows each bot's connection, the rooms that count against its limit (linking to their referee pages) and the lines it sent in the last window, live over `bancho` events.
- One task owns everything (`manager/`): the bots' connections, every room's state and every running match (unconcluded with a room). It takes what the bots hear, what the pages ask (through the `Bancho` handle in `AppState`, over a channel) and the site's changes (`Live::changes`) in order, so there's never a race for who runs a room or saves a score. Each room's state is a fold of BanchoBot's lines (`room/`, `RoomState::apply`, unit-tested on real lines), so a restart reads it back from the saved chat and the bots rejoin their rooms. A match's context (players and where they belong, the pool, bans, picks, hand-awarded results, "Sayma", options, the saved score; `context.rs`) is read again whenever the pages are told that match changed, a `bracket` change resyncs everything, and every running match is read again each minute anyway; role changes and dev reseeds resync too.
- Every line in a room's channel, the bot's own included, is saved to `room_chat_messages` by that room's bot only (one writer task, in order; `bot_id` on the row) and pushed as `chat`. The manager pushes each changed room's view (`view.rs`, `LiveRoom`: every room with its bot and `access`, the latest room's state, the pool map on, every finished map read against the pool with each team's total and the winner, the tally, whether the site has it) at most every 200 ms as `room`. Rows are keyed by `mp_id`, not by match, so a room's lines before it's on its match are kept too.
- Load balancing: Bancho lets an account be in at most `ROOM_LIMIT` (4) rooms at once, counting the ones it was made a referee of, so a bot is never added to another bot's room. A new room goes to the connected bot with the fewest rooms open (`pick_bot`; one `!mp make` at a time per bot, since BanchoBot's answer doesn't say which request it's for); when every bot with room to spare is busy making one, the request waits in a queue and goes to the first bot that's free (`queued`, given up after `QUEUE_TIMEOUT`, a minute), so rooms due at the same time all open within seconds; when every bot is at the limit the request is refused. A bot BanchoBot says can't make more is left out for 15 minutes and the room is tried on the next one. `match_rooms.bot_id` says which bot runs each room (`bot_made` when it made it; a room made elsewhere and added by link goes to the least busy bot, which joins once the room's maker `!mp addref`s it) until `closed_at`. A concluded match's room is closed by its bot 30 seconds after the referee concludes it (`CLOSE_AFTER_CONCLUDED`; concluding pushes `bracket`, so the bots see it at once, and reopening within that time keeps the room), and one replaced by a newer room 15 minutes after (`CLOSE_AFTER_REPLACED`); a room the bot didn't make is left instead, so forgotten rooms don't hold the bots' slots; one taken off its match or whose match is deleted goes at once. If a room's bot drops, the room waits for it; the referee can move the match to a new room with another bot.
- What a room does by itself, in the match's latest room only: `!mp set` (Team Vs for bracket matches, Head to Head for qualifiers; the score mode, Score V2 by default; size = player count), `!mp lock`, an `!mp invite #<osu_id>` per player and `!mp settings` when it's made; `!mp settings` as each map starts so every player's mods are read; moving bracket players to their slot and their team's colour as they join (the room option "Girenleri yerine ve takım rengine taşı"; `move_commands`/`place_commands` in `room/commands.rs`: a slot another player holds is swapped through a free one, the room growing by one when it's full and shrinking back, and an outsider in a match slot is moved past them; each move ends with `!mp settings`, since Bancho barely answers `!mp team`; each join's moves are worked out from the room as it was then, so once all the match's players are in, 3 s after the last join and once the bot has nothing left to send, it checks everyone's slot and colour again and moves anyone still out of place: up to 3 times, 10 s apart, counted again from the next join (`reposition` in `manager/automation.rs`)); kicking whoever joins without belonging there ("Maç dışından girenleri at", on by default, and only once the staff are loaded; a join under a name no member has asks for `!mp settings` first and is settled by the osu! id it gives, so a player who renamed on osu! is placed rather than kicked); making every holder of Referee who joins the room in-game one of its referees (never a bot); and putting the tiebreaker on (the score line, `!mp map` and `!mp mods`) once the score reaches it, once per score, between maps, unless the referee put another map on. It also counts the score (`tally/`) and saves it through `services::bracket::save_score` whenever it changes (a map finishes, a result is awarded, a map is left out, an osu! sync), not on the first reading after it starts or the match is loaded, so a score a host entered by hand stands until something changes it (or the referee saves); saves go out one at a time, in order.
- The bots keep a pace below what Bancho allows (`connection/bot.rs`): at most 6 lines in any 5 s and never two within 400 ms, a referee's command before automation before refreshes, rooms taking turns within each, at most 40 lines waiting per room, one `!mp settings` per room waiting (a new one goes to the back), and nothing that waited through a disconnect is sent after it.
- To try it locally without Bancho, point `BANCHO_IRC_ADDR` at a stand-in IRC server; the dev accounts' negative ids work in every command.
- The bots' IRC passwords are stored encrypted (`secret.rs`, ChaCha20-Poly1305 with a random nonce, `bancho_bots.password_nonce`/`password_ciphertext`) under `BOT_SECRET_KEY` (64 hex digits). Without the key no password can be saved (`bot_secret_missing`); a row saved before encryption (`irc_password`) is encrypted at startup once the key is set, and a password sealed with another key can't be read, so that bot stays offline until a host enters it again.
- The manager, its chat writer and score saver, and the room score reader run under `supervise::spawn` (`supervise.rs`; so do the Discord worker, scheduler and gateway, the rank refresh, the cache invalidation and the session cleanup): a panic is logged and the task starts again after 2 s. The manager's inbox is a `SharedReceiver`, so the new one reads on where the old one stopped and rebuilds the rooms from their saved chat as at startup; the old one's bot connections close as their handles drop.
- The auto referee's waits are its own (`autoref/countdown.rs`); it never uses Bancho's `!mp timer` (a referee's "Zamanlayıcı" in manual mode still does), only `!mp start 10` to start a map. Each wait (the no-show wait, a warmup turn, the roll, a team's ban or pick, a map's ready time and the NoFail grace) is a `Countdown`: the room is told how long it has, then at every whole minute left, at 30 s and 10 s, and when it's over. The bot acts `GRACE` (10 s) after the end it announced, and each phase's deadline (`until`) is the countdown's. Every line a bot sends comes back as it goes out (`BotEvent::Line`, with the time it was written), and the manager hands its own lines to `AutoRef::said`: a countdown line that went out late moves the end back by as long, so what the room was told is true from when it saw it. Closing a finished match's room works the same in the manager (`CLOSE_WARN_TEXT` 10 s before, `CLOSED_TEXT` as it closes, `close_at` `GRACE` after the end told, moved back when the closing lines go out late).
- The auto referee (`autoref/`, a pure state machine with unit tests; driven by `manager/auto_referee.rs`, which feeds it a `Snapshot` of the match with every BanchoBot line, every player line in the latest room and a 1 s clock, and does the `Action`s it returns): every match runs on it unless a referee switches it to manual (`match_room_settings.auto_ref`, default on; `PUT /api/matches/:id/autoref`, `services::autoref::set_enabled`). Its state (phase, timers, each team's `-abort` uses) is saved after every change (`match_autoref`, through an ordered writer with the log), so a restart, the room's bot coming back, or a switch back from manual (after a referee's switch or a `-durdur`) picks up where the match stands (`resume`, run by `drive` the first time the auto referee runs the match again, `Run.auto_live`: the phase's timers start over and whatever happened meanwhile is read from the room and the match: a pool map being played is followed to its end; a match taken past its start by hand (a ban, pick, missed turn, played map, or the first turn decided) skips the warmups and roll; a pick marked by hand is played; a pool map a referee put on during a team's pick, unmarked, is taken as that pick if the team may pick it). A pool map started by someone else while the bot waits between maps (`draft`, `next`, a lobby's `waiting`) is followed too. The room hears every switch between auto and manual (`reload` in `manager/runs.rs`), unless the bot switched itself and said why (`Run.auto_told_off`). While the hosts have it on (`app_settings.auto_rooms`, off by default; the switch on `/admin/bancho`, `PUT /api/bancho/auto-rooms`), the manager opens the room of every unconcluded match in auto mode 10 minutes before its time (`OPEN_BEFORE`; a bracket match with both teams, a lobby with a team, no room yet, not more than 30 minutes past; `db::autoref::due_rooms`, checked every 15 s, retried after a minute), with the usual set-up and invites; Discord is then told the room's bot (`Job::RoomAdded { bot }`). Phases: `waiting` (at the match's time the missing players are invited again and the room told; everyone in and the time come, or everyone ready earlier, starts it; ten minutes after the time, or after the room was made if later, a bracket team that isn't complete forfeits (`services::autoref::forfeit_no_show`) and a lobby's incomplete teams are kicked and taken out of the lobby (`lobby_no_show`), their players told on the site and by DM; nobody complete hands the match over), then for a bracket match `warmup` (each team in turn has 3 minutes to choose a warmup on the room page, nobody getting the room's host: `Web::Warmup`, the beatmap checked against the turn first (`check_warmup`), then described by osu! off the manager's loop (`describe_warmup`; length, set, mode) and handed back as `Request::PlayerWarmup`; a pool song (the map, or another difficulty of its set), a map over 4 minutes or not osu!standard is refused to the page; the bot puts it on with `!mp map` and Freemod; once all four are ready on it, or the turn's time runs out (at least a minute after it was chosen), it starts with `!mp start 10`; one a referee puts on is still checked and aborted if it breaks the rules; "skip" on the page skips, even before the team's turn) and `roll` (each team's first `!roll` up to 100, 2 minutes; a tie rolls again, one missing loses, both missing is random; the winner is team 1 of the stage's order, set as `next_pick_team_id`), `draft` (a player of the team whose turn `turn_of` says holds the map for 3 seconds on the room page, `Web::Draft`; nothing typed in the room counts; tiebreaker, banned, picked or played maps are refused (`refusal`, a `PlayerError` back to the page), and so is a pick in the same category as the team's previous pick unless NM; then `services::bracket::mark`. On the clock: `BAN_TIME` 3 min, `PICK_TIME` 2 min; one that runs out is `Action::Missed`, recorded by `services::bracket::miss`: a ban is lost, a pick passes to the other team, and a passed pick that runs out again hands the match over; while it waits, a turn the site no longer gives that team, changed by a referee, is asked again), `ready` (the map and mods, 120 s in a bracket, 180 s in a lobby; all ready (a bracket needs all four in the room) or the time running out starts it with `!mp start 10`, a FM/TB map first reading `!mp settings` and warning whoever lacks NF, starting anyway once the timer ran out again after the warning; on an FM map (not TB) each team needs a player on one of HD, HR, EZ or FL (`FM_MODS`): a team without one is warned the same way, and still without one once the timer ran out again, the map doesn't start and the match is handed over, back to `next` so a switch back opens it afresh), `playing` (`-abort` or a player dropping in the first 30 s aborts on the team's account, 2 each; in a lobby a drop only counts on its first map), `scoring` (3 s after the map, osu!'s record of the rooms is read like a referee's sync, up to 5 tries, else the chat's scores stand), `next` (a lobby's next unplayed pool map, or a bracket match's tiebreaker, winner or next turn; the old automatic tiebreaker cue is off in auto mode) and `done` (the winner or "every map played" said, then `autoref::CLOSING`, the match concluded with the counted score so the room closes `CLOSE_AFTER_CONCLUDED` later). With `DEV_LOGIN` on (`Snapshot::stand_ins`), the dummy accounts (negative osu! ids), who can't join a room, count as in it and ready (`present`: the no-show wait, a bracket map's wait for all four, the warmup's), the room page doesn't list them as missing (`AutorefView.stand_ins`), the NoFail and FM checks only read the players in the room anyway, and a team of only dummies rolls by itself as the roll opens, never the other team's number (`stand_in_rolls`), so one real player can test a match alone; they never score, so the real player's team wins each map. Anything it can't settle (no best of, an order run out, a score over the best of) is a handover: the match goes to manual and the staff are told (`services::autoref::handover`). Players' commands, typed in the room or done on the room page (`POST /api/matches/:id/player`, `handlers/player.rs`: `PlayerAction` to the manager as `Request::Player`, `AutoRef::web`, whose refusal is a `PlayerError` the page shows instead of a room line; bans, picks and warmups only there): `-abort`, `-rapor <text>` (also in manual mode; once a minute, 300 characters) and `-durdur` (any player, any number of times, once a minute each; also in manual mode: a match in auto mode goes to manual, `services::autoref::emergency_stop`, until a referee switches it back) notify every host and referee (`notifications` kinds `player_report`/`emergency_stop`, `autoref_stopped` for handovers) and ping the referee channel (`Job::AutorefAlert`); `-davet` in a private message to the room's bot invites the player again (every 20 s at most). While it runs a match it keeps the room locked (`keep_locked`: `!mp lock` whenever the room's state isn't locked, every 30 s at most, `LOCK_EVERY`); and a bracket player BanchoBot says changed slot or colour by themselves (not within 5 s of the bot's own lines, `OWN_MOVE_WINDOW`) has the room's places checked again as after a join. The bot's announcements of a warmup turn and of a ban or pick carry the room page's link (`Snapshot.page`, `room_page_url`). Every decision is written to `autoref_logs` (`services::autoref::log`) and sent as `autoref_log` (staff watching the match); `LiveRoom.autoref` (`AutorefView`) says where it stands.

# Bancho bots (`backend/src/bancho`)

- Everything that speaks to Bancho is here: the site's bots, which this section is about, and each referee's own connection for talking (`relay.rs`, above). The site's own osu! accounts make, run and log every multiplayer room. Hosts add them on `/admin/bancho` (`pages/admin/BanchoAdminPage.tsx`; `handlers/bancho.rs`: `GET`/`POST /api/bancho/bots`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/bancho/bots/:id`) with the account's IRC password (`bancho_bots`; write-only, never sent back) and switch each on or off; every active one stays connected (`bot.rs`: logs in, answers PINGs, reconnects with backoff, and waits for a new password after Bancho refuses one). The page shows each bot's connection, the rooms that count against its limit (linking to their referee pages) and the lines it sent in the last window, live over `bancho` events.
- One task owns everything (`manager.rs`): the bots' connections, every room's state and every running match (unconcluded with a room). It takes what the bots hear, what the pages ask (through the `Bancho` handle in `AppState`, over a channel) and the site's changes (`Live::changes`) in order, so there's never a race for who runs a room or saves a score. Each room's state is a fold of BanchoBot's lines (`room.rs`, `RoomState::apply`, unit-tested on real lines), so a restart reads it back from the saved chat and the bots rejoin their rooms. A match's context (players and where they belong, the pool, bans, picks, hand-awarded results, "Sayma", options, the saved score; `context.rs`) is read again whenever the pages are told that match changed, a `bracket` change resyncs everything, and every running match is read again each minute anyway; role changes and dev reseeds resync too.
- Every line in a room's channel, the bot's own included, is saved to `room_chat_messages` by that room's bot only (one writer task, in order; `bot_id` on the row) and pushed as `chat`. The manager pushes each changed room's view (`view.rs`, `LiveRoom`: every room with its bot and `access`, the latest room's state, the pool map on, every finished map read against the pool with each team's total and the winner, the tally, whether the site has it) at most every 200 ms as `room`. Rows are keyed by `mp_id`, not by match, so a room's lines before it's on its match are kept too.
- Load balancing: Bancho lets an account be in at most `ROOM_LIMIT` (4) rooms at once, counting the ones it was made a referee of, so a bot is never added to another bot's room. A new room goes to the connected bot with the fewest rooms open (`pick_bot`; one `!mp make` at a time per bot, since BanchoBot's answer doesn't say which request it's for); when every bot is at the limit the request is refused. A bot BanchoBot says can't make more is left out for 15 minutes and the room is tried on the next one. `match_rooms.bot_id` says which bot runs each room (`bot_made` when it made it; a room made elsewhere and added by link goes to the least busy bot, which joins once the room's maker `!mp addref`s it) until `closed_at`. A concluded match's room is closed by its bot 30 seconds after the referee concludes it (`CLOSE_AFTER_CONCLUDED`; concluding pushes `bracket`, so the bots see it at once, and reopening within that time keeps the room), and one replaced by a newer room 15 minutes after (`CLOSE_AFTER_REPLACED`); a room the bot didn't make is left instead, so forgotten rooms don't hold the bots' slots; one taken off its match or whose match is deleted goes at once. If a room's bot drops, the room waits for it; the referee can move the match to a new room with another bot.
- What a room does by itself, in the match's latest room only: `!mp set` (Team Vs for bracket matches, Head to Head for qualifiers; the score mode, Score V2 by default; size = player count), `!mp lock`, an `!mp invite #<osu_id>` per player and `!mp settings` when it's made; `!mp settings` as each map starts so every player's mods are read; moving bracket players to their slot and their team's colour as they join (the room option "Girenleri yerine ve takım rengine taşı"; `move_commands`/`place_commands` in `commands.rs`: a slot another player holds is swapped through a free one, the room growing by one when it's full and shrinking back, and an outsider in a match slot is moved past them; each move ends with `!mp settings`, since Bancho barely answers `!mp team`); kicking whoever joins without belonging there ("Maç dışından girenleri at", on by default, and only once the staff are loaded; a join under a name no member has asks for `!mp settings` first and is settled by the osu! id it gives, so a player who renamed on osu! is placed rather than kicked); making every holder of Referee who joins the room in-game one of its referees (never a bot); and putting the tiebreaker on (the score line, `!mp map` and `!mp mods`) once the score reaches it, once per score, between maps, unless the referee put another map on. It also counts the score (`tally.rs`) and saves it through `services::bracket::save_score` whenever it changes (a map finishes, a result is awarded, a map is left out, an osu! sync), not on the first reading after it starts or the match is loaded, so a score a host entered by hand stands until something changes it (or the referee saves); saves go out one at a time, in order.
- The bots keep a stricter pace than a referee's own connection (`bot.rs`): at most 6 lines in any 5 s and never two within 400 ms, a referee's command before automation before refreshes, rooms taking turns within each, at most 40 lines waiting per room, one `!mp settings` per room waiting (a new one goes to the back), and nothing that waited through a disconnect is sent after it.
- To try it locally without Bancho, point `BANCHO_IRC_ADDR` at a stand-in IRC server; the dev accounts' negative ids work in every command.
- The bots' IRC passwords are stored encrypted (`secret.rs`, ChaCha20-Poly1305 with a random nonce, `bancho_bots.password_nonce`/`password_ciphertext`) under `BOT_SECRET_KEY` (64 hex digits). Without the key no password can be saved (`bot_secret_missing`); a row saved before encryption (`irc_password`) is encrypted at startup once the key is set, and a password sealed with another key can't be read, so that bot stays offline until a host enters it again.
- The manager, its chat writer and score saver, and the room score reader run under `supervise::spawn` (`supervise.rs`; so do the Discord worker, scheduler and gateway, the rank refresh, the cache invalidation and the session cleanup): a panic is logged and the task starts again after 2 s. The manager's inbox is a `SharedReceiver`, so the new one reads on where the old one stopped and rebuilds the rooms from their saved chat as at startup; the old one's bot connections close as their handles drop.
